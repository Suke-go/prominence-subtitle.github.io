**Key finding**: Emotional design works best for *medium-difficulty* content. For easy or hard content, motivation boost doesn't translate to better learning outcomes.

## B. Kinetic Typography: Engagement & Learning Outcomes

| Treatment | Effect | Theoretical Basis |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Moving text (well-paced) | 3× better memory than static | Sequential presentation mirrors oral language flow; activates "shared thinking process" between instructor & learner |
| Per-word animation (scale pop, bounce) | Rapid attention capture + rhythm | Each word gets "voice"; transforms literacy into orality |
| Synchronous color/size with prosody (volume, stress) | Externalizes audio dynamics | Listener doesn't have to infer stress/emotion from voice alone — text shows it visually |
| Over-animated text (constant motion) | Cognitive fatigue + comprehension ↓ | Violates segmentation principle; split attention between content & motion |

**Best practice**: Motion density <30% of text; balance animated elements with static pauses for processing.

## F. Aesthetic-Usability Effect & Hedonic Quality (Hassenzahl/AttrakDiff)

**Core finding**: People perceive more aesthetic designs as more intuitive, even if actual usability is identical (cognitive bias).

**AttrakDiff dimensions**:
- **Pragmatic quality (PQ)**: Usability, efficiency, accuracy
  - For captions: correctness, readability, sync accuracy
- **Hedonic quality — Stimulation (HQ-S)**: Novelty, surprise, visual variety
  - Animated color pulses, dynamic per-word pop, emoji augmentation
- **Hedonic quality — Identity (HQ-I)**: Self-expression, personal resonance, values alignment
  - Customization, tone-matching, culturally-sensitive markers
- **Attractiveness (ATT)**: Overall beauty/appeal

**Design implication for captions**: Even if subtitle accuracy is constant, adding visual playfulness (animations, warm colors, emojis) increases perceived quality & likelihood of re-viewing.

## G. Visual Treatment Techniques & Expected Effects

### Low Cognitive Load (Static or Minimal)
- Plain text, occasional bold/italic
- **Effect**: Fast comprehension, low fatigue
- **Use case**: Simple/low-difficulty content

### Medium (Recommended for Motivation Boost)
| Technique | Visual | Effect |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| **Per-word pop** | Word scales from 0→100% + bounce easing | Attention capture, rhythm, playfulness |
| **Color pulse** | Text/background hue cycles or saturation increases | Emotional engagement, beat synchrony |
| **Karaoke highlight** | Baseline color → warm hue as word spoken | Prosody visibility, reading support |
| **Proportional sizing** | Font size ∝ volume/stress in speech | Audio dynamics made visible |

### High (Risk of Overload)
- Simultaneous rotation + scale + color + spatial drift
- **Effect on learning**: Cognitive fatigue; memory *of the animation* replaces memory *of content*
- **Acceptable only for**: High-energy, entertainment-focused content (music videos, ads)

## H. Recommended Measurement Scales for Study

### 1. **Primary: Motivation**
- **Intrinsic Motivation Inventory (IMI) — 16-item short form** (7-point Likert)
  - Subscales: interest/enjoyment, perceived competence, value/usefulness, pressure absence
  - Time: 5 min post-video
  - *Why*: Gold standard in learning research; captures intrinsic motivation directly

### 2. **Engagement & Retention**
- **Watch time** (% of total video watched)
- **Completion rate** (% who watch to end)
- **Skip rate** (% who leave before 25% mark)
- **Replay segments** (# of times user replays specific section)
- *Why*: Platform-independent; correlates with learning gains

### 3. **Emotional Response**
- **Self-Assessment Manikin (SAM)** — 3D (visual, 1-9 scales)
  - Valence (unhappy ← → happy)
  - Arousal (calm ← → excited)
  - Dominance (controlled ← → in control)
- **Positive Affect scale** (subset of PANAS): 5 items (enthusiastic, interested, proud, etc.)
- *Why*: Validates that emotional design actually elicits positive emotion

### 4. **Learning Outcomes**
- **Retention test** (24h delay): Free recall + recognition of key concepts
- **Transfer test**: Apply knowledge to new scenario
- **Comprehension**: Multiple-choice or short-answer on main ideas
- *Why*: Measures whether motivation → actual learning

### 5. **Purchase Intent** (if applicable)
- **3-item scale** (7-point Likert):
  - "I would purchase this product"
  - "I would recommend this to a friend"
  - "This product meets my needs"
- *Why*: Direct measure of persuasion effect

### 6. **Hedonic Quality & Beauty**
- **AttrakDiff** (30-item, 7-point bipolar scale)
  - Yields PQ, HQ-S, HQ-I, ATT scores
  - *Alternative (shorter)*: Add 3-5 beauty/appeal items to SUS
- *Why*: Captures whether animated captions are perceived as *fun & engaging* beyond mere accuracy

### 7. **Cognitive Load**
- **NASA-TLX** (6-item; 3-subscale short form)
  - Mental demand, effort, frustration
- *Why*: Ensures that kinetic typography doesn't *reduce* learning by overloading

### 8. **Practical Outcomes**
- **Video completion at key milestones** (0-10s, 10-30s, 30-60s, final)
  - Reveals *when* viewers drop off
- **Brand/product recall** (post-video, unaided): "Name a product you saw" → measure accuracy
- **NPS-style recommendation**: "Would you share this video?" (0-10)

### Measurement Timeline

1. **Pre-video**: Demographic, prior knowledge, baseline mood (SAM)
2. **During**: Eye-tracking (optional), frame-by-frame attention map
3. **Immediately post**: Affect (SAM, PANAS), perceived difficulty (NASA-TLX)
4. **5 min post**: IMI, learning comprehension check
5. **24h post**: Retention test, transfer test
6. **Post-study**: AttrakDiff (full UI evaluation), recommendation likelihood

## K. Risk Factors & Mitigation

| Risk | Symptom | Mitigation |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Cognitive overload** | Watch time ↓, frustration ↑ | Motion density <30%; use silence/pauses for processing |
| **Animation distraction** | Recall of animation > recall of content | Reserve movement for key concepts only; foreground content with motion, background static |
| **Attention splitting** (muted video) | Viewer misses both audio & caption timing cues | Kinetic typography *must* lead visually (pop *before* audio event, not after) |
| **Age/literacy mismatch** | Younger learners benefit more; older may find "cutesy" design patronizing | Test anthropomorphism separately; warm colors universally acceptable |
| **Cultural sensitivity** | Emoji/symbols may not translate across regions | Pre-test with target demographics; provide customization options |
