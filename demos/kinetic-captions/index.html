Onomatope engine: syllable nuclei, onset sharpness (plosive-ness), noisiness (fricative-ness) and nucleus length are measured directly from the band-passed energy envelope, spectral flux and zero-crossings — no dictionary, the same DSP that will run in WASM/SIMD in real time. A syllable moves only if it stands out from its ±1.5 s neighbourhood (local-contrast z, phonetic figure–ground); the strong syllable of a prominent word gets a squash-and-stretch spring (sharper onsets snap harder, long nuclei stretch wider, noisy onsets shiver), weak syllables recede. The line itself stays level: every motion is mean-zero and settles home.
